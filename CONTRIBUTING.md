# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this project. This repository
contains public, openly available work on the Functor Category Model
Architecture (FcMA) and related materials. Contributions, corrections, and
substantive discussion are welcome.

## How to reach us

For anything not covered below — questions, proposals, or discussion — email
**jharby@functormodel.ai**. Please put a clear, specific subject line on your
message so it can be routed and answered.

## Ways to contribute

- **Corrections.** Errors in the mathematics, proofs, documentation, or code —
  from typos to substantive mistakes. Precise corrections with a clear
  description of the problem are especially valued.
- **Clarifications.** Places where the exposition is unclear, incomplete, or
  could be made more accessible without loss of rigor.
- **Questions and discussion.** Good-faith technical questions about the work
  are welcome and often improve the material for everyone.
- **Extensions.** Proposed additions or generalizations that build on the
  public work.

## How to propose a change

1. **Open an issue first** for anything beyond a trivial fix, describing the
   problem or proposal clearly. This lets us discuss the approach before effort
   is spent.
2. **Keep changes focused.** One logical change per contribution is easier to
   review than several unrelated ones bundled together.
3. **Explain the "why."** State what the change fixes or improves and, where
   relevant, how you verified it.
4. **For mathematical or proof contributions,** state the claim precisely and
   distinguish what is proven from what is conjectured or empirical.

## Standards

- Be precise. Claims should be stated at the level they are actually supported —
  distinguish proven results from empirical observations and from conjecture.
- Be respectful and constructive. Disagreement about technical substance is
  welcome; keep it about the work.
- Cite sources where a contribution draws on external results.

## Scope

This repository covers **public** material only. It does not include, and this
process does not extend to, any proprietary, patent-pending, or otherwise
restricted work. Please do not submit material that is confidential or subject
to third-party rights, and do not request access to non-public components
through this channel.

## Licensing

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be incorporated into this
public work. If a contribution is significant, we will discuss appropriate
attribution. If you are unsure whether a contribution is compatible with the
project's licensing, ask first at **jharby@functormodel.ai**.

## Reporting a problem privately

If you believe you have found something that should be raised privately rather
than in a public issue, email **jharby@functormodel.ai** directly rather than
opening a public issue, and describe the concern clearly.
